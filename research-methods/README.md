# Research Methods

Personally-authored skills for the mechanics of doing and writing up
quantitative research — general methodology and manuscript craft that is not
specific to health technology assessment (`hta/`), biostatistics
(`biostatistics/`), or clinical ML (`ml-clinical/`).

## Skills

- **[scientific-writing](./scientific-writing/)** - Structure, draft, and
  critique scientific manuscripts as arguments: thesis-first framing, IMRaD
  discipline, paragraph craft, converting bullet skeletons to prose,
  structured abstracts, and manuscript-wide cohesion checks.
- **[grilling](./grilling/)** - Relentless round-by-round interview to
  stress-test a plan, decision, or analysis approach. Entry points are
  **[grill-me](./grill-me/)** (no files written) and
  **[grill-with-docs](./grill-with-docs/)** (also builds the glossary and
  decision records).
- **[to-spec](./to-spec/)** - Turn the conversation into a working spec for
  an analysis or for software, with no further interview.
- **[domain-modeling](./domain-modeling/)** - Build and sharpen a project's
  glossary and its decision records (ADRs, analysis-plan deviations).

The last five are adapted from
[mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) (MIT); each
directory carries its LICENSE.

## Common Use Cases

- Turning an outline or bullet skeleton into manuscript prose
- Auditing a draft's argument (thesis, promises, section jobs) before
  circulating it to co-authors
- Writing or tightening an abstract, discussion, or results narrative
- Stress-testing a plan or analysis approach before committing to it
- Writing up a settled discussion as a spec, glossary entry, or ADR
