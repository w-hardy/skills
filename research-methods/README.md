# Research Methods

Personally-authored skills for the mechanics of doing and writing up
quantitative research — general methodology and manuscript craft that is not
specific to health technology assessment (`hta/`), biostatistics
(`biostatistics/`), or clinical ML (`ml-clinical/`).

## Skills

- **[scientific-writing](./scientific-writing/)** - Structure, draft, and
  critique scientific manuscripts as arguments: thesis-first framing, IMRaD
  discipline, paragraph craft, converting bullet skeletons to prose,
  structured abstracts, and manuscript-wide cohesion checks.

## Common Use Cases

- Turning an outline or bullet skeleton into manuscript prose
- Auditing a draft's argument (thesis, promises, section jobs) before
  circulating it to co-authors
- Writing or tightening an abstract, discussion, or results narrative
